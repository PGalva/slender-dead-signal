Event log

    ■ Objective

    You operate a night-vision camera across a 5×5 forest grid. The operation runs as 3 back-to-back sectors — clear each one's pages and the next begins instantly, with no return to the menu. Recover all pages before the camera static hits 100% or the battery dies. You start in the corner (1,1); the Entity starts in the opposite corner, (5,5), and it does not wander — it hunts.

    ■ The 3 Sectors

    Each sector is a new environment, a new map, and a faster hunt
    SectorEnvironmentPagesThreat
    1The Dark Pine Forest2Tutorial pace — the Entity often loses your trail.
    2The Abandoned Shack3Faster hunt, quicker battery drain.
    3The Forgotten Monolith3Extreme speed, high static, fast drain — relentless.

    Battery carries between sectors (with a +22% recharge at each new one), and the Entity grows more aggressive the more sectors you've cleared this run.

    ■ Core Loop

    1. Move 1 cell per action with WASD, the arrow keys or the on-screen D-pad.
    2. The flashlight reveals nearby cells: Empty, 🔋 Battery (+20%) or 📄 Page (+1).
    3. The battery drops per move you make — there is no background timer, so the pace is yours (but the Entity only rests while you do).
    4. After each step, the Entity takes a step toward you; collecting pages raises its double-step chance. Get its page target and the sector clears with a glitch transition into the next.
    5. All 3 sectors cleared = victory. Static at 100% or battery at 0% = Signal Lost.

    ■ Die? Retry the Sector

    Death does not send you back to Sector 1. The game-over screen offers ↻ Retry Sector — it restarts only the sector you fell in, instantly, with the battery you entered it with, so you keep your momentum. ⟲ Restart Run begins the whole operation again.

    ■ Survival Strategy

    • It always closes the distance, so never flee in a straight line into a corner — you'll get trapped. Circle around it and win space back.
    • Only distance 3+ recovers static strongly (distance 2 just eases it). Plan routes that buy a few cells of slack after each scare.
    • Each page makes the Entity faster, and every cleared sector makes it hungrier. The final pages of Sector 3 are the most dangerous — save battery packs for them.
    • Hitting a wall costs no battery and doesn't move the Entity — a blocked move isn't a step.
    • Turn 🔊 Sound on: the heartbeat races as static climbs — an audio cue for how close it is even off-camera.
