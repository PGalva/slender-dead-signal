Event log

    ■ Objective

    You operate a night-vision camera in a forest laid out as a 5×5 grid. Recover the 8 journal pages before the camera static reaches 100% or the battery runs dry. You start in the corner (1,1); the Entity starts in the opposite corner, (5,5).

    ■ Core Loop

    1. Move 1 cell per action with WASD, the arrow keys or the on-screen D-pad.
    2. The flashlight reveals the contents of nearby cells: Empty, 🔋 Battery (+20%) or 📄 Page (+1).
    3. The battery drops 1% per move you make — there is no background timer, so the pace is yours.
    4. After each of your steps, the Entity takes 1 step toward you. As you collect pages, its chance of a double step grows.
    5. 8 pages = victory. Static at 100% or battery at 0% = end of the watch.

    ■ Survival Strategy

    • It always closes the distance, so never flee in a straight line into a corner — you'll get trapped. Circle around it and win space back.
    • Distance 2 eases the static a little; only distance 3+ recovers it strongly. Plan routes that buy a few cells of slack after each scare.
    • Each page makes the Entity faster (double step). The last pages are the most dangerous — save the battery packs for them.
    • Hitting a wall costs no battery and doesn't move the Entity — a blocked move doesn't count as a step. The Entity only moves when you actually move.
    • The camera shows the threat's bearing in the center of the D-pad. Let it guide your escape.
